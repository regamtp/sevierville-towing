Frequently Asked Questions

Are you really available 24 hours?

Yes — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, including holidays.

How fast can you reach me?

It depends on your exact location and current road conditions, but because we're local to Sevierville, we're often nearby. We'll give you a realistic arrival time when you call.

What if I don't know exactly where I am?

Look for a mile marker, a nearby business, an intersection, or a cabin or rental company name, or share your location from your phone. We'll help you figure it out on the call.

Do you tell me the price before you start?

Yes. We give you an upfront price based on your location, vehicle, and destination. No surprise charges after the job is done.

What kinds of vehicles can you handle?

Cars, trucks, SUVs, motorcycles, campers and RVs, and heavier equipment. If you're unsure, call and describe it and we'll tell you straight.

Can you tow inside the national park?

Commercial towing inside Great Smoky Mountains National Park is restricted and generally requires Park Service involvement. Tell us your location and we'll coordinate — it's normal for us, but it adds time.
