Sevierville is where the Smokies meet the interstate, and that changes what towing here involves. Winfield Dunn Parkway carries six divided lanes from I-40 Exit 407 down into town, so Sevierville towing means highway-speed collisions and loaded RVs at one end and courthouse-square parking calls at the other. Sevierville Towing runs crews across both, dispatching from our Sevierville base a few miles south on US 441.

What Towing in Sevierville Actually Involves

I-40 Exit 407 & the Diverging Diamond

Almost every vehicle entering Sevier County from the north comes through one interchange. Exit 407 was rebuilt as a diverging diamond in 2015, and while it moves far more traffic than the old design, the crossover geometry still catches drivers who've never seen one — especially at night, towing a trailer, after six hours on the interstate. Accident towing concentrates here and on the ramps, and the vehicles are frequently loaded: fifth-wheels, travel trailers, box trucks, and coaches. Rotator-capable equipment handles the jackknifed trailers and overturned box trucks, and the flat ground here is an advantage — we set up outriggers properly instead of working off a grade.

Winfield Dunn Parkway (SR 66)

SR 66 is the spine — 8.6 miles of six divided lanes, entirely within Sevierville city limits, from the interchange down to the US 441/US 411 junction downtown. Constant turning movements and summer volume turn a single breakdown into a mile of backup, so getting the vehicle out of live traffic is the first priority. Most of our Sevierville calls come off this corridor.

The Outlet & Retail Corridor

The Tanger Outlets and Governor's Crossing lots produce a steady run of parking-lot calls — dead batteries after a long afternoon, keys locked inside, and slow leaks from curb strikes that we can usually fix without a full tow. Retail lots along the parkway are also a regular private-property account, handled on a coordinated basis with the property, never as a surprise tow.

Douglas Lake & the Back Roads

Toward Douglas Lake, the terrain gets gentler than Gatlinburg's, so grade calls here are more about mud and loose gravel near the boat ramps than steep switchbacks. The boat-and-camper crowd and the RV dealers along US-441 generate their own mix of trailer recoveries and lakeside lockouts.

Do you tow at the Exit 407 interchange?

Yes. Accident towing concentrates at the diverging diamond and on the ramps. Give us your exit number and direction of travel and we'll come to you.

Yes. Dead batteries and lockouts at Tanger Outlets and Governor's Crossing are among the most common calls we get in Sevierville.

Do you do authorized towing from retail lots?
