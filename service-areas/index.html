Where We Serve

Sevierville is home base. The city, its roads and communities, Highway 66, Seymour, and the Dandridge edge of I-40 are covered on this site.

Where the Trucks Run

Sevierville Towing covers these roads from Sevierville. The truck comes to the vehicle. Each town, road, and community below has its own page on seviervilletowing.com, and those pages link back here.

Sevierville

Sevierville is the county seat and the home base. Highway 66, Winfield Dunn Parkway, runs from I-40 Exit 407 south to the Forks of the River. US-441 leaves that junction toward Pigeon Forge. Calls range from ramp wrecks at the diverging diamond to dead batteries in shopping rows and stalls around the courthouse. Towing in Sevierville.

Downtown Sevierville

Downtown is the south end of Highway 66: the courthouse, Bruce Street, Court Avenue, and the junction of US-441 and US-411 at the Forks of the River. Streets are tighter than the parkway, and a truck has to stage without blocking the bridge over the Little Pigeon River. Towing in downtown Sevierville.

Highway 66

Highway 66 is the divided parkway from that downtown junction to Exit 407, about 8.6 miles, all inside the Sevierville city limits. The outlet lots, Governor's Crossing, and the diverging diamond sit on this road. It is the corridor page, separate from the square. Towing on Highway 66.

Pigeon Forge

The Parkway through Pigeon Forge is a slow commercial strip on summer afternoons and a through-route the rest of the year. Vehicles stall in attraction queues, in resort lots off the main drag, and near the north end where traffic squeezes toward the Spur. Reaching the car usually means a side drive or a lot entrance, not a straight shot down the through lanes. Towing in Pigeon Forge.

Gatlinburg

Gatlinburg sits in a narrow valley. The Parkway, River Road, and the streets that climb toward the ski area are tight, and the south end of town is the US-441 entrance to Great Smoky Mountains National Park. Grades, tour buses, and people on foot change how a truck stages. If the vehicle is inside the park, say so. Commercial towing there is limited and usually goes through the Park Service, which adds time. Towing in Gatlinburg.

Kodak

Kodak is the community north of the French Broad River along Highway 66, at I-40 Exit 407. Breakdowns cluster on the diverging-diamond ramps, the travel-center lots, and Douglas Dam Road. It is the gateway into the county, still Sevierville Towing. Towing in Kodak.

Seymour

Seymour is the Chapman Highway community south of Sevierville, on the Sevier-Blount county line. US-441 here is a commuter road with thin shoulders, not the six-lane parkway. Boyd's Creek is the stretch between Seymour and town. The truck still comes from Sevierville. Towing in Seymour.

Wears Valley

Wears Valley Road, US-321, runs west from Pigeon Forge toward Townsend. Cabin drives and campground lanes turn off it. The pavement is narrower than the Parkway, and a lot of the places a vehicle actually sits are gravel, sloping, or shared with parked guests. Winching and careful spotting matter more here than they do in a flat retail lot. Towing in Wears Valley.

Dandridge edge

Dandridge is in Jefferson County, east of Kodak on I-40. The regular edge is Exit 407 to Exit 412 at Deep Springs Road and Exit 415, where US-25W and US-70 leave the interstate and enter Dandridge. We do not cover all of Jefferson County from this page. If the pin is farther toward White Pine or Jefferson City, say so and we will tell you before a truck is promised. Towing on the Dandridge edge.

Sevierville roads and communities

These are smaller pages on the same dispatch. Each one is a road or a community with its own landmarks, not a second copy of the city page.

Dolly Parton Parkway

US-411 east of the square. East Main becomes the parkway within a couple of blocks, passes the high school side of town, and meets Veterans Boulevard and Air Museum Way. Towing on Dolly Parton Parkway.

Veterans Boulevard

SR-449, the bypass opened in 2006. The Sevierville miles run from Dolly Parton Parkway across Middle Creek to Collier Drive. The Pigeon Forge end of the same road stays on the Pigeon Forge page. Towing on Veterans Boulevard.

Middle Creek

LeConte Medical Center, the county emergency room, is at 742 Middle Creek Road and open every hour. The bypass reused parts of the old alignment, so the road name has to be specific. Towing on Middle Creek Road.

Gists Creek

Signal 17.4 on Highway 66 is the turn for 202 Gists Creek Road, the Sevierville Convention Center, and the golf club beside it. Towing on Gists Creek Road.

Sevierville Airport

Gatlinburg-Pigeon Forge Airport sits in Sevierville at 134 Air Museum Way. It is a general-aviation field with hangars and the Tennessee Museum of Aviation, not an airline terminal. Towing at the Sevierville airport.

Boyds Creek

Boyds Creek Highway, SR-338, leaves Chapman Highway in Seymour and runs northeast to the parkway. Boyds Creek Elementary is on that two-lane. Towing in Boyds Creek.

Douglas Dam

The TVA dam on the French Broad, about five miles north of Sevierville, reached on Douglas Dam Road from the Kodak stretch of Highway 66. Boat ramps and a two-lane, not the I-40 exits. Towing at Douglas Dam.

Alder Branch

Farmland on Old State Highway 66, the SR-338 alignment between Sevierville and the French Broad crossing just west of the dam. Towing in Alder Branch.

Harrisburg

The 1875 Harrisburg Covered Bridge over the East Fork of the Little Pigeon, a short way down Harrisburg Road from SR-339. The truck stages on the approach. Towing in Harrisburg.

Old Newport Highway

SR-339, with the Walters State Sevier County campus at 1720 Old Newport Highway and the turns for Harrisburg and Jones Cove. Towing on Old Newport Highway.

Cherokee Hills

The residential stretch of US-411 at the east edge of Sevierville, between the parkway and Fairgarden. Towing in Cherokee Hills.

Fairgarden

A census-designated place of a few hundred people on Newport Highway, about nine miles east of downtown. Fairgarden Road and South Flat Creek Road leave the highway here. Towing in Fairgarden.

Jones Cove

Jones Cove Road climbs off Old Newport Highway past Jones Cove Elementary toward Bethel Church Road. Towing in Jones Cove.

Richardson Cove

Pittman Center Road along the Middle Prong, from the fork with Birds Creek past the fire department and the McMahan Cantilever Barn. Towing in Richardson Cove.

Caton's Chapel

A foothill community on Caton's Chapel Road near Pittman Center Road, with Caton's Chapel Elementary. Dolly Parton went to school here. Towing in Caton's Chapel.

Roads that cross those towns

A handful of routes account for a large share of the calls, whichever town name is on the pin:

  • I-40 at Exit 407 (Kodak and Highway 66), then east through Exit 412 to Exit 415 at the Dandridge edge.
  • Highway 66, Winfield Dunn Parkway, from Exit 407 south to the Forks of the River downtown.
  • Chapman Highway, US-441, from downtown Sevierville through Boyd's Creek to Seymour.
  • US-441 from Sevierville through Pigeon Forge, then the Spur into Gatlinburg.
  • Veterans Boulevard (SR-449), the bypass east of the Parkway between the Sevierville side and Pigeon Forge.
  • US-321, Wears Valley Road, toward Townsend.
  • Dolly Parton Parkway, US-411, east from downtown through Cherokee Hills toward Fairgarden.
  • Old Newport Highway, SR-339, to the Walters State campus, Harrisburg, and Jones Cove.
  • Pittman Center Road, SR-416, from US-411 through Richardson Cove.
  • Boyds Creek Highway, SR-338, from Seymour to the parkway, then Old State Highway 66 through Alder Branch.
  • Douglas Dam Road, SR-139, from Highway 66 in Kodak to the TVA dam.
  • Veterans Boulevard, SR-449, from Dolly Parton Parkway across Middle Creek.

What to tell dispatch

The town name is only a start. The details that matter are the road or the lot, the direction you were heading, what the vehicle is, whether it rolls and steers, and where you want it taken. If you are on a shoulder, a ramp, or a steep drive, say that before the make and model. Call (865) 277-9406. A non-urgent question can go through the contact form, but a form does not dispatch a truck.
