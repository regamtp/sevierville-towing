The RV parks spread throughout Sevier County keep rigs moving in and out constantly, and a breakdown on the highway or in a campsite needs equipment actually rated for a 40-foot Class A or a loaded fifth-wheel. Sevierville Towing brings the right rig for the job. Call (865) 381-0045 and describe what you're driving.

What to Do While You Wait

Once you've called, a few things keep you safer until the truck arrives. If you can do so safely, move your vehicle out of the travel lane and onto a shoulder. Turn on your hazard lights so approaching drivers see you early. On a busy road it's usually safer to step away from the vehicle and wait behind a barrier. Keep your phone handy — your driver may call to pin down exactly where you are.
